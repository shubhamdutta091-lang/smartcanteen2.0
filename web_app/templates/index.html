SmartCanteen: ML-Based Food Demand & Waste Reduction

A machine learning web platform empowering institutional canteens to forecast daily student dining volume, recommend precise batch quantities with automated 10% safety stock buffers, eliminate food wastage, and prevent meal shortages.

🎯 Objective & Problem Statement

Institutional college canteens deal with severe demand volatility driven by exam periods, weekly schedule patterns, weather shifts, and campus attendance. Without predictive analytics, kitchen managers rely on guesswork, resulting in:

  • Massive Surplus Food Waste: 15% to 35% of freshly cooked meals get dumped into trash bins on low-attendance days.
  • Meal Shortages (Stockouts): Canteens run out of staple lunch items during rush hours, disappointing hungry students.
  • Severe Financial Loss: Significant budget leakage on raw food ingredients, dairy, cooking gas, and trash disposal.

🌟 Core Stakeholder Benefits

👨‍🍳 Canteen Chefs

Automated cooking batch sizes with safety buffer.

🎓 Students & Faculty

Guaranteed meal availability & fresh hot food.

🚀 High-Impact Operational Benchmarks

  • ✓ Forecasting Accuracy: Up to 98.1% R² Score
  • ✓ Food Waste Cut: Reduces surplus by up to 60%
  • ✓ Automated Safety Buffer: 10% Shortage Stock Buffer
  • ✓ Multi-Meal Support: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner
  • ✓ Context Awareness: Exams, Holidays, Rain, Temperature

Architecture Pipeline Flow

Data Collection (Attendance, Weather, Sales) → Preprocessing & Validation → Feature Engineering (Lags 1 & 7, 7d/14d Rolling Avgs) → Chronological Time Split → ML Model Training (Linear, DT, RF, XGBoost) → Automated 10% Safety Buffer → Kitchen Batch Sizing & Audit Report.

📌 Methodology & Machine Learning

SmartCanteen employs supervised time-series regression algorithms leveraging yesterday's lag sales, 7-day and 14-day rolling averages, student campus attendance, exam periods, and weather signals.

Algorithms Benchmarked:

  • Random Forest Regressor (Champion): 98.1% R² Score, MAE 9.71
  • XGBoost Regressor: 97.4% R² Score, MAE 10.51
  • Decision Tree Regressor: 96.2% R² Score, MAE 13.04
  • Linear Regression: 93.3% R² Score, MAE 17.82
